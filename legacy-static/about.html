Our Purpose

What is LSA?

Legit Source Analysis (LSA) is a football match analysis platform dedicated to providing transparent, research-based insights into football matches across major leagues and competitions.

We focus on presenting factual information, statistical data, and analytical perspectives to help football enthusiasts better understand the factors that influence match outcomes. Our platform serves as an educational resource for those interested in the analytical side of football.

Our Methodology

Our Analytical Approach

Comprehensive analysis through multiple data points and expert evaluation

01
📈

Form Analysis

We examine recent team performance over the last 5-10 matches, analyzing win/loss records, goal-scoring patterns, defensive stability, and momentum trends.

02
📊

Statistical Review

Our statistical analysis includes goals scored and conceded, possession percentages, shots on target, expected goals (xG), and home/away records.

03
🔄

Head-to-Head History

Historical matchups between teams often reveal patterns and tendencies. We examine previous encounters and notable tactical factors.

04
🏥

Team News & Context

Injuries, suspensions, tactical changes, managerial situations, and motivational factors all influence match outcomes.

05
⚽

Tactical Considerations

Different tactical setups and playing styles create various match dynamics. We analyze how teams approach games and their strengths.

06
👁️

Multiple Perspectives

Rather than a single conclusion, we highlight multiple perspectives and scenarios. Football is complex and unpredictable.
